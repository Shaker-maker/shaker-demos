Methodology

Assessment → Hardening → Ongoing Monitoring

Every engagement runs through the same three-phase loop. You get a documented finding set, a prioritized remediation plan, and continuous visibility — not a one-time report that gathers dust.

PHASE 01

Assessment

Black-box and grey-box testing across your external surface, internal apps, cloud configuration, and human layer. Every finding is severity-ranked with a reproducible proof of concept.

  • External attack surface mapping
  • OWASP-aligned VAPT
  • Cloud config review
  • Phishing simulation baseline
PHASE 02

Hardening

Direct collaboration with your engineers and vendors to close findings. We don't hand over a PDF and disappear — we sit in the standup until the critical findings are verified closed.

  • Prioritized remediation roadmap
  • Patch verification retest
  • ODPC alignment documentation
  • Staff training rollout
PHASE 03

Ongoing Monitoring

Continuous external scanning, alerting on new exposures, and quarterly posture reviews. You get a running scoreboard your board can actually read.

  • Continuous external scans
  • New-domain / leaked-credential alerts
  • Quarterly executive reporting
  • Incident-response retainer
